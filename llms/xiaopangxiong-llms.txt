# Xiaopangxiong (小胖熊)

> Xiaopangxiong ("small fat bear") is a Chinese B2B e-commerce platform for home and office renovation materials, operated by Shanghai Miju Network Technology Co., Ltd. (上海米居网络科技有限公司). It connects building-material suppliers with renovation contractors, foremen, and decoration companies, selling the full range of auxiliary materials (辅材) — sand, cement, paint, wiring, pipes, tiles — through a self-operated model spanning factory-direct procurement, warehousing, logistics, delivery, installation, and after-sales service.

Xiaopangxiong is headquartered in Shanghai (Jing'an district) with service stations and warehouses in Suzhou, Hangzhou, Hefei, Ningbo, Wuxi, and Jiaxing. The company is backed by SoftBank Vision Fund 2 and distributes primarily through its 小胖熊 mobile app (Android package com.nmm.smallfatbear). It publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, or open-platform program. Its web properties are hosted in mainland China and are generally unreachable from networks outside China.
